RASD discovers, authenticates and controls every machine on your wired network in real time — no agents lost, no Wi-Fi trusted, no command unaccounted for.

SCROLL
01 · DISCOVERY

Authenticated discovery

Agents find the admin over the LAN with an HMAC-signed handshake. A spoofed "I'm the admin" packet is rejected and logged — no hijacking.

02 · ENFORCEMENT

Wired-LAN-only

Wi-Fi, VPNs and virtual adapters are refused for control traffic. Every socket is bound to a real Ethernet interface, enforced on both sides.

03 · CONTROL

Real-time command tracking

Restart, shutdown, lock USB — every command is queued, delivered and acknowledged, with the full trail visible from issue to confirmation.

04 · POLICY

USB & device control

Block mass-storage the instant it's inserted, per endpoint or fleet-wide, with a forensic record of every device event.
